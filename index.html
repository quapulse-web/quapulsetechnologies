2024 – 2026
Founder, Ngaoyi Cleaning

Operating a multi-regional property service venture across residential and commercial maintenance, Bonga worked at the front lines of the built environment. Here he encountered a recurring systemic failure: the property industry trapped in a cycle of "Reactive Loss" — spending millions to repair damage that could have been predicted and prevented.

The Pivot
From Reaction to Prediction

Recognising that cleaning up after a catastrophe was an inefficient solution to a data problem, Bonga pivoted his focus toward Proactive Mitigation. He leveraged his background in operational management and deep expertise in electronics prototyping to develop Acoustic Waveguide Impedance Mapping™ — a proprietary multi-modal sensor fusion architecture that assigns a continuous acoustic and thermodynamic signal profile to every monitored asset, enabling statistically validated pre-failure classification.

Today
